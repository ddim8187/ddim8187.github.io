How to use it

  1. Upload a track: click the box above or drag a file in.
  2. Shape the tone with EQ: pick a Character preset to start, then fine-tune the 7 bands.
  3. Add drive: raise it for a touch of warm analog-style harmonics.
  4. Tame harshness with De-harsh: raise it if vocals or hi-hats sound piercing.
  5. Adjust stereo width: widen or narrow the image.
  6. Raise loudness: set the ceiling and push amount to bring up the overall level.
  7. Download the WAV: once you're happy, press "Download Mastered WAV".

What this tool does

It runs your track through EQ, drive (saturation), de-harsh, stereo width and loudness (a limiter) in one pass, then lets you download the result as a WAV file. Everything happens in your browser (the Web Audio API) — nothing is uploaded to a server.

What each stage does

7-band EQ (Sub·Bass·L-Mid·Mid·H-Mid·Pres·Air)

Boost or cut seven bands from 60Hz (low-end weight) up to 12kHz (air and clarity). Pick a Character preset as a starting point, then fine-tune from there.

Drive (saturation)

Adds a touch of nonlinear distortion for warm, analog-style harmonics. Push it too far and things get harsh, so dial it in gradually.

De-harsh

Gently dips around 3.8kHz to smooth out piercing high end — useful when vocal sibilance or hi-hats feel too sharp.

Stereo width

Widens or narrows the difference between the left and right channels (the side signal). 100% is the original; lower values move toward mono with a more centered image.

Loudness (Maximizer)

Momentarily clamps the signal so it never exceeds the chosen ceiling, raising overall loudness. "How much to push" controls how hard it's driven.

Being upfront about the meter

The on-screen meter isn't the broadcast/streaming LUFS standard (ITU-R BS.1770, which includes K-weighting and gating) — it's a simple RMS-to-dBFS reference reading. If you need an accurate loudness value for distribution, double-check with a dedicated loudness meter before finalizing.

FAQ

Is my uploaded file stored anywhere?

No. It's processed entirely in your browser's memory and is never sent to or stored on a server. It disappears when you close the tab.

Why does the download button take a moment?

At download time, the entire track is re-processed from start to finish at full precision to build the file. This can take anywhere from a few seconds to a bit longer depending on the track's length.

Will the result sound as good as a paid mastering service?

It won't match a professional mastering engineer or a large AI-trained service. That said, it's plenty useful for quickly polishing a rough mix or finishing a demo-quality track.

Do I need to sign up or log in?

No. You can use it right away without any account.

What file formats can I upload?

Most formats your browser can play — MP3, WAV, M4A, and others. If a file won't load, try converting it to WAV or MP3.

What format is the downloaded file?

A 16-bit WAV file. It's lossless, so you can archive it or edit it further in another program without quality loss.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works in mobile browsers too, though processing speed depends on your device and the track's length.

Good to know

  • Playback and processing may not work with every browser/file combination. If that happens, try a widely supported format like WAV or MP3.
  • Large or long files take longer to process and may feel sluggish depending on your device.
  • This tool is provided for reference only; it doesn't guarantee the sound quality or fitness for any particular purpose.
