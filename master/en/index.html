What This Tool Does

Polish music created with Suno AI and other tools using EQ, Drive (saturation), De-harsh, Stereo Width, and Loudness Control (limiting), then download the processed result as a WAV file. All processing happens in your browser using the Web Audio API; files are never sent to a server.

What Does Each Stage Do?

7-Band EQ (Sub · Bass · L-Mid · Mid · H-Mid · Pres · Air)

Adjust seven frequency bands from 60 Hz (low-end weight) to 12 kHz (air and clarity). Start with the Tone Character dropdown, then fine-tune as needed.

Drive (Saturation)

Drive adds subtle nonlinear saturation and warm harmonics reminiscent of analog equipment. Increase it gradually, as too much can make the sound harsh.

De-harsh

It gently reduces harsh frequencies around 3.8 kHz. Try it when vocal sibilance or hi-hats sound too sharp.

Stereo Width

Adjust stereo width by increasing or reducing the difference between the left and right channels. 100% is the original width; lower values move the sound toward mono and strengthen the center.

Loudness Control (Maximizer)

It increases overall loudness while keeping short peaks below the selected ceiling. Use Output Gain to control the amount.

About the Level Meter

The on-screen meter is not a formal LUFS measurement using the ITU-R BS.1770 standard with K-weighting and gating. It is a reference value based on simple RMS converted to dBFS. For precise delivery loudness, verify the final file with a dedicated loudness meter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are uploaded files saved anywhere?

No. Files are processed only in your browser memory. They are not sent to or saved on a server and disappear when you close the tab.

Why does downloading take some time?

The song is rendered from start to finish again when you download it. Depending on the song length, this may take a few seconds to several tens of seconds.

Will the result match a paid mastering service?

It will not match the precision of a professional mastering engineer or large AI-based service. However, it can be useful for quickly polishing rough mixes, demos, and guide tracks.

Do I need to sign up or log in?

No. You can use it without signing up or logging in.

Can I use MP3 or WAV files from Suno?

Most audio formats supported by your browser, including MP3, WAV, and M4A, should work. If playback fails, convert the file to WAV or MP3 and try again.

What format is the downloaded file?

The download is a 16-bit WAV file. WAV is lossless, making it suitable for archiving or further editing in another program.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. It works in smartphone and tablet browsers. Processing speed may vary depending on your device and file length.
